# Contoso business travel policy (fictional)

## Booking
Book all trips through the travel desk. Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours.

## Daily allowance
The daily allowance for meals is 40 EUR in Portugal and 60 EUR abroad. Keep receipts for anything above the
allowance; the excess needs manager approval.

## Expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days of the trip in the HR portal.
