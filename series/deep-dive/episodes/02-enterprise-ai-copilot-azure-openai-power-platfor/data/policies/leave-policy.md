# Contoso annual leave policy (fictional)

## Entitlement
Full-time employees get 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees get a
pro-rata amount based on their contracted hours.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March,
otherwise they are lost.

## Requesting leave
Request leave in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance. Your manager approves or rejects the request
within 3 working days.
