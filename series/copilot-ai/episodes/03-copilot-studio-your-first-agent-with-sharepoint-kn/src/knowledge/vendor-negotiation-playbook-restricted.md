# Contoso Vendor Negotiation Playbook (Restricted)

This document is for the Vendor Management Office only. It contains target prices and must not be shared with vendors.

## Target discounts

On hosting renewals the VMO aims for a discount of 12 percent. On printing and travel renewals the target is 5 percent.
The walk-away point is a price increase of more than 8 percent.
