# Contoso Vendor Renewals and Offboarding

## Renewals

The VMO keeps a vendor register with the renewal date of every contract. The budget owner decides at least 90 days
before the renewal date whether the contract is renewed, renegotiated or ended. If nobody decides, the VMO escalates to
the budget owner's manager 60 days before the date.

## Offboarding

When Contoso stops using a vendor, the budget owner opens an offboarding task. Access is removed, data is returned or
deleted and the vendor is closed in the vendor register. The VMO confirms the closure by email.
