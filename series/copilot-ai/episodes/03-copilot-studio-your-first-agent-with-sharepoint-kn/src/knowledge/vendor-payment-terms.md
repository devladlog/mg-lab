# Contoso Vendor Payment Terms

Accounts payable (AP) pays vendors every Thursday for invoices that are due that week.

## Standard terms

Contoso pays approved invoices 30 days after the invoice date. A vendor may ask for shorter terms; the procurement
manager can agree to 14 days if the vendor offers a discount of at least 2 percent.

## Invoice requirements

An invoice must show the Contoso purchase order number, the vendor tax number and the amount in euros. Accounts payable
only pays invoices that carry a purchase order number and an approval from the budget owner.

## Disputes

If an invoice is disputed, the budget owner tells accounts payable within 5 working days. Disputed amounts are not paid
until the dispute is closed.
