# Contoso Vendor Onboarding Policy

This policy applies to every external supplier that Contoso pays for goods or services. The Vendor Management Office
(VMO) owns it. Version 3, valid from 1 January.

## When a vendor request is needed

Every new vendor needs a vendor request before the first purchase order or contract is signed. A renewal of an existing
vendor with a changed scope also needs a new request.

## Approval tiers

The approver depends on the estimated yearly cost of the vendor in euros:

| Estimated yearly cost | Approver |
|---|---|
| Below 5,000 euros | Team lead |
| 5,000 to 24,999 euros | Procurement manager |
| 25,000 euros and above | Finance director, after a security review |

A request that is split into smaller parts to stay below a tier is treated as one request.

## What the request must contain

The requester gives the vendor name, a category (hosting, printing, travel, security or other), the estimated yearly
cost and a short business justification. The VMO gives the request a number in the format VR-0001.

## Timing

Contoso aims to finish onboarding within 10 working days of a complete request. A request without a justification is
returned to the requester and the clock does not start.
