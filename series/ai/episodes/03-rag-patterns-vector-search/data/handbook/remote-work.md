# Hybrid and remote work

<!-- Contoso Ltd employee handbook. Fictional sample content for the DEVLADLOG AI series. -->

## Hybrid working pattern

Office-based roles work at least 2 days per week in the office. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days when whole
teams are expected on site. Team leads can agree a different pattern for customer-facing teams.

## Working from abroad

You can work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Submit form HR-31 at least 15
working days in advance; HR checks tax and social security rules before approving. Working from abroad without an
approved HR-31 is not allowed.

## Home office allowance

Every employee on a hybrid contract can claim a one-off home office allowance of 300 euros for a desk, chair or
monitor. Claim it through the expenses tool with the receipt within 60 days of purchase.

## Ergonomics and wellbeing

Book a free ergonomic assessment through the HR portal. Take a short break away from the screen at least every
hour, and use the wellbeing hub for confidential support.
