# Code of conduct

<!-- Contoso Ltd employee handbook. Fictional sample content for the DEVLADLOG AI series. -->

## Gifts and hospitality

You can accept gifts from suppliers or customers worth up to 50 euros. Register any gift above 50 euros in the
gift register with form CMP-7 within 5 working days, and ask compliance whether you may keep it.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any personal relationship or financial interest that could influence a business decision. Use form CMP-2
and tell your line manager before you take part in the decision.

## Speaking up

Report suspected fraud, harassment or safety issues to your line manager, to HR or through the confidential ethics
hotline. Contoso doesn't tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
