# IT equipment and devices

<!-- Contoso Ltd employee handbook. Fictional sample content for the DEVLADLOG AI series. -->

## Laptop refresh

Company laptops are replaced every 3 years, or earlier if IT confirms a hardware fault. IT contacts you when your
device is due; you don't need to raise a request.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT service desk and to security within 24 hours. IT wipes the
device remotely. A police report is required for stolen devices before a replacement is issued.

## Personal devices

You can read work email and use Teams on a personal phone only after enrolling it in the company device
management app. Never store customer data on an unmanaged device.

## Leaving Contoso

Return your laptop, phone, badge and accessories to IT on your last working day. Equipment that isn't returned is
deducted from the final salary at its current book value.
