# Leave policy

<!-- Contoso Ltd employee handbook. Fictional sample content for the DEVLADLOG AI series. -->

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a
pro-rata allowance based on their contracted hours. New joiners accrue leave from their first working day.

## Requesting leave

Request annual leave with form HR-12 in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day off. Your line
manager approves or declines the request within three working days. Requests for more than 10 consecutive working
days also need approval from your department head.

## Carry-over

You can carry up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March;
any carried-over day still unused after 31 March is lost. Days above the 5-day limit are not paid out.

## Sick leave

Tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. You can self-certify up to 3 consecutive calendar
days of sickness. From the fourth day you need a medical certificate, uploaded to the HR portal.

## Parental leave

Contoso tops up statutory parental pay to 100 percent of base salary for the first 16 weeks. Tell HR at least
8 weeks before the planned start date so payroll can be adjusted.
