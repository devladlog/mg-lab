# Travel and expenses

<!-- Contoso Ltd employee handbook. Fictional sample content for the DEVLADLOG AI series. -->

## Submitting expense claims

Submit expense claims in the expenses tool within 30 days of the expense. Attach a receipt for every item above
25 euros. Supplier invoices must show Contoso's VAT number, the supplier's VAT number, the invoice date and the
tax amount, otherwise finance returns the claim.

## Meals and per diem

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 40 euros per day for domestic trips and 60 euros per day abroad.
Alcohol is never reimbursed. Meals that are already included in a conference fee can't be claimed again.

## Flights and trains

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or more, premium economy is allowed with
manager approval. Prefer the train when the door-to-door journey takes less than 4 hours.

## Mileage

Business trips in your own car are reimbursed at 0.36 euros per kilometre. Commuting between home and your usual
office is not a business trip.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment needs pre-approval under policy EXP-4 when the expected cost is above 150 euros. Record the
names of the guests and the business purpose on the claim.
