# Learning and development

<!-- Contoso Ltd employee handbook. Fictional sample content for the DEVLADLOG AI series. -->

## Training budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per calendar year for courses, books and certification exams.
Your line manager approves spending from the budget; unused budget doesn't carry over.

## Learning days

You get 5 paid learning days per year in addition to annual leave. Book them in the HR portal like leave, with the
category "learning".

## Certifications

Contoso pays the first attempt of a certification exam from your learning budget. A retake is paid by Contoso only
if the exam is required for your role.

## Conferences

Conference tickets come from the learning budget. Travel to the conference follows the travel and expenses policy.
